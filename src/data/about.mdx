I am a software and machine learning engineer working at the intersection of software
engineering and applied research in computer vision and vision-language models. I hold an
M.Sc. in Computer Science from [ETH Zürich](https://inf.ethz.ch/), and I currently work as a
Machine Learning Data Engineer at Ethon in Zürich, building scalable, production-ready
systems for developing and evaluating machine-learning models.

Before Ethon, I was a Machine Learning Engineer at Ucentrics AG, where I built training and
inference pipelines, LLM-based document-understanding tools, and synthetic data generation
for 3D and real-time visual inspection. Earlier I was a Research Assistant in ETH Zürich's
Product Development Group, and I completed my Master's thesis at Disney Research | Studios,
designing efficient transformer networks for topology-independent shape modeling. Before my
Master's, I spent three years at Oracle in Bengaluru — first building cloud software for
supply-chain processes at the Oracle Applications Lab, and later taking deep-learning NLP
models to production as cloud services at Oracle Cloud AI Services.

I earned my B.Tech. in Electrical Engineering from the
[Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee](https://www.iitr.ac.in/). My earliest research,
as an intern at the Technical University of Munich, focused on deep-learning segmentation
of infant-brain MRI.

My work spans computer vision, vision-language models, and efficient, production-ready deep
learning. Take a look at my [publications](/publications/) and [projects](/projects/), or
read my [CV](/cv/).
