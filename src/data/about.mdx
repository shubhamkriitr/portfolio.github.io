I am a computer scientist working in machine learning and computer vision. I hold an
M.Sc. in Computer Science from [ETH Zürich](https://inf.ethz.ch/), where I am currently a
Research Assistant in the Product Development Group, developing few-shot visual anomaly
detection models for industrial component inspection.

I completed my Master's thesis at Disney Research | Studios in Zürich, where I designed
efficient transformer networks for topology-independent shape modeling. Before my Master's,
I spent three years at Oracle in Bengaluru — first developing cloud software to streamline
supply-chain processes at the Oracle Applications Lab, and later taking deep-learning NLP
models to production as cloud services at Oracle Cloud AI Services.

I earned my B.Tech. in Electrical Engineering from the
[Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee](https://www.iitr.ac.in/). My earliest research,
as an intern at the Technical University of Munich, focused on deep-learning segmentation
of infant-brain MRI.

My work spans medical image analysis, 3D vision, and efficient deep learning. Take a look at
my [publications](/publications/) and [projects](/projects/), or read my [CV](/cv/).
