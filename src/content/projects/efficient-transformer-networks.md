_Neural Network Optimization Group @ Disney Research | Studios & Computer Graphics Lab, ETH Zürich · March 2023 – September 2023_

To handle a large number of input tokens efficiently, a more efficient variant of attention-based models is needed, with a reasonable trade-off between performance and efficiency. In this work, a novel attention mechanism is developed, which is tailored to the requirements of shape modeling and processing point clouds. It shows improved results on modeling hand-poses compared to efficient attention models like XCA and LUNA. The proposed method also allows the user to inject prior information directly into the attention module, which enables artistic control of very articulated motion.
